## Stakeholder Relationships

### Primary Decision-Makers
- **Bill Murphy (ADA)** — controls the $1M purse and the operating lead hire. The AI pilot
  must make Murphy's operating lead more effective, not replace them.
- **Oklahoma Department of Commerce** — holds the funding amendment gate (G1). Their
  replicability concern is our product angle: ThinkSpace-in-a-box.

### Coordination Partners
- **Chamber President** — the natural coordination interface for founders. The toolkit must
  be handoff-able: no standing technical support from Chamber staff.
- **Amanda Dion (COO)** — operationally hands-on; the best day-to-day contact for keeping
  the pilot grounded in ADA's actual constraints.

### Strategic Partners
- **CORI** — strategy author and operating lead search partner. Contract runs through
  August 30. The toolkit must align with CORI's evidence-gated approach.
- **REI Oklahoma, SBDCs** — existing partners. The outreach positioning (hermes-83hq.2)
  explicitly frames AI ThinkSpace as complementary to these partners, not competitive.
